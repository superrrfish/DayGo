# DayGo

**DayGo** is a transit and commute planning web app built for ShellHacks: think Google Maps meets Google Calendar. Instead of just giving directions, DayGo tells you when to leave based on your destination, desired arrival time, and mode of transport (car, rideshare, transit, walking, or biking).

## How it works
Give DayGo a destination, an arrival time, and how you're getting there, and it calculates your ideal departure time, automating the transit math that people do manually every day.

## Roadmap
Deeper integration between planning and transit (routine scheduling, calendar sync) is coming soon, closer to how Notion works for planning your day, not just your commute.

## Tech stack
- **Backend:** Java + Spring Boot
- **Frontend:** HTML/CSS/JavaScript
